## What I do

I'm a full-stack developer, which in practice means I like owning a product end to end. I can sit with a founder and work out what the real problem is, sketch how the product should feel, design the database, write the frontend and backend, deploy it to production and then keep improving it once real people use it.

My day-to-day tools are React, Next.js, TypeScript and Node.js, with Supabase, Firebase and MySQL for data, and Linux, Nginx and Vercel for infrastructure. I also take technical SEO seriously. A product nobody can find doesn't help anyone.

## CTO & Co-founder of VagMed India

[VagMed India Private Limited](https://vagmed.com) is a Jaipur-based company building anatomy-aware, biodegradable menstrual hygiene products for different life stages — teens, adults and mature women. I'm the company's CTO and one of its co-founders.

The company's consumer brand is [WythHer](https://wythher.com). As CTO I own its technology. I built the wythher.com storefront with Next.js on Vercel and a full D2C admin platform for orders, inventory and content. Checkout runs on Razorpay plus cash on delivery. I also handle analytics and market analysis.

Working on a femtech brand taught me that technology is never the whole product. Trust, accessibility and tone matter as much as page speed — and a checkout that supports COD can matter more than any animation.

## My book: The Last Conversation

I'm also an author. ***[The Last Conversation](https://store.pothi.com/book/chandan-pal-the-last-conversation/)*** is a 113-page psychological drama novel, published on Pothi.com. It's a story about love, loss and memory — about the conversations that end too soon, and the people we never forget.

Writing a book and building software turn out to be similar disciplines: cut what doesn't serve the reader, respect their time, and rewrite until it feels effortless.
